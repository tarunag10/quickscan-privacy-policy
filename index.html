QuickScanToPDF Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 May 2026

QuickScanToPDF is designed to scan documents into PDFs while keeping your files private and under your control.

Information We Collect

QuickScanToPDF does not collect, sell, or share personal information. The app does not require an account and does not include advertising or third-party analytics.

Camera Access

The app asks for camera access so you can scan paper documents. Camera images are used only to create scans and PDFs inside the app. They are not uploaded to our servers.

Document Storage

Scanned documents and generated PDFs are stored on your device by default. You can delete your saved PDFs from inside the app.

Optional iCloud Sync

If you choose to enable iCloud sync, your documents may be stored in your personal iCloud account and synced by Apple across your devices. iCloud syncing is optional and controlled by your device and Apple ID settings.

Sharing PDFs

When you share or export a PDF, the app uses the standard iOS sharing tools. You choose where the file goes and which apps or services receive it.

Children's Privacy

QuickScanToPDF is not designed to collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy if the app changes. The latest version will be available on this page.
